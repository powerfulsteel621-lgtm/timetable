🐱 All About Cats

A very quick site about the world's finest roommates.

Why cats?

Cats are quiet, self-cleaning, and mostly nocturnal — which makes them excellent companions for anyone who values a tidy home and a warm lap.

Meet the breeds

Maine Coon

Enormous, fluffy, and surprisingly gentle. Often described as "dog-like" in temperament.

Siamese

Talkative and deeply attached to their people. Expect commentary on everything you do.

British Shorthair

Round face, plush coat, calm demeanour. The teddy bear of the cat world.

Bengal

Spotted, athletic, and endlessly curious. Needs plenty of play and vertical space.

Cat facts

  • Cats sleep 12–16 hours a day.
  • A group of cats is called a clowder.
  • Purring may help bones and tissue heal.
  • A cat's nose print is unique, like a human fingerprint.
  • Cats have 32 muscles in each ear.

Caring for a cat

  1. Fresh water daily, in a wide bowl away from food.
  2. One litter box per cat, plus one spare.
  3. Scratching posts — tall enough for a full stretch.
  4. Ten minutes of play, twice a day.
  5. Yearly vet checkups.
